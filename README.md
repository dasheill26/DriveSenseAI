DriveSense AI

Overview

DriveSense AI is an AI-powered vehicle diagnostics and predictive maintenance platform developed as a Final Year Software Engineering project at Bournemouth University.

The system combines OBD-II telemetry, rule-based reasoning, fault-code analysis, live vehicle monitoring, and conversational AI to help drivers understand vehicle health and maintenance requirements through clear and actionable insights.

⸻

Problem Statement

Modern vehicles generate large volumes of telemetry and diagnostic data through sensors, ECUs and onboard systems. Existing diagnostic tools often present technical information that can be difficult for everyday drivers to understand.

DriveSense AI bridges the gap between complex vehicle diagnostics and non-technical users by transforming fault codes and telemetry data into understandable explanations and maintenance recommendations.
