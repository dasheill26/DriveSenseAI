⚠ Limitations

Honest evaluation
Real vehicle OBD integration is planned but kept until the final stage to avoid breaking stable features.
Machine learning predictions are prototype-level and should support, not replace, professional inspection.
Image diagnostics depend on image quality and should not be treated as guaranteed fault confirmation.
Demo data is used when live OBD connection is unavailable.
